What moves the price up or down on your home

  • System size: the biggest driver. We size to your actual bill and daytime usage, not to the biggest system we can fit, so you are not paying for panels that only feed the grid at 5 cents a kWh.

  • Panels and battery brand: Jinko Tiger Neo panels in the packages above, Australian Made Tindo panels as an upgrade built for coastal salt and heat. Fox ESS, Tesla Powerwall 3, Sigenergy and Enphase batteries, each priced on your quote.

  • Your roof and switchboard: two-storey or tile roofs, shading that needs optimisers, three-phase supply or an older switchboard can add cost. We check all of it on site so the fixed price covers it, and you never get a variation on install day.

What comes off: the solar STC rebate, the Federal Battery Rebate at about $252 per usable kWh (falling to about $211 per usable kWh for installs from 1 January 2027), and the NSW VPP incentive if you choose to join a virtual power plant. All three are handled by us and deducted before you see the price.

Questions people ask about solar system cost

How much does a solar system cost on the Central Coast in 2026?
Our published packages run from $9,720 after rebates for 6.65 kW of solar with a 14 kWh battery, to $23,985 for 13.3 kW with two Tesla Powerwall 3 batteries. A solar-only system is priced on your quote. The free on-site assessment turns these into a fixed price for your roof.
Why do quotes from different companies vary so much?
Panel and battery brand, whether the install is subcontracted, what is left out of the quote and added on the day, and whether the rebates are shown before or after. Our quote is fixed after an on-site check, uses named products, and has every rebate already deducted.
Is a battery worth the extra cost?
For most Central Coast homes on a time-of-use tariff, yes, while the Federal Battery Rebate is at about $252 per usable kWh. It also keeps the lights, fridge and Wi-Fi on in a storm. We show the payback on your quote using your actual bill, and if a battery does not stack up for you we say so.
What rebates come off the price?
The solar STC rebate on the panels, the Federal Battery Rebate at about $252 per usable kWh (dropping to about $211 per usable kWh from 1 January 2027), and the NSW VPP incentive if you join a virtual power plant. All are deducted on the quote and lodged by us.
Can I pay it off?
Yes. Coastal Solar Solutions is an approved vendor for the NSW Home Energy Saver Program: a 0% interest loan of up to $15,000 over up to 10 years for households with a combined taxable income of $210,000 or under, with no early-payout penalty. Rebates come off the bill before the loan applies. Ask during your free assessment and we will show you the weekly repayment against your current power bill.
Is the assessment really free, and is there a catch?
Free, no obligation, and no hard sell. A licensed installer visits, measures, and sends a fixed quote within 24 hours. If you go elsewhere, you still leave with an honest system size for your home.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Coastal Solar's installers are licensed electricians and every install meets Australian Standards and CEC requirements. [CONFIRM: licence numbers and public liability cover] Every job is done by our in-house crew, never subcontractors.
