NSW VPP incentive

The NSW upfront battery rebate ended on 30 June 2025 and was replaced by the Federal program. What NSW still pays is a one-off incentive, up to about $1,500 depending on battery size and provider, for signing your battery up to a virtual power plant. It stacks with the Federal rebate. Questions people ask about the battery rebate

Is there still a NSW battery rebate?
The NSW upfront battery installation rebate ended on 30 June 2025 and was replaced by the Federal Battery Rebate, which is bigger. NSW still pays a one-off incentive for connecting your battery to a virtual power plant, worth up to about $1,500 depending on battery size and provider, and it stacks with the Federal rebate. We put both on your quote and tell you honestly whether the VPP is worth it for you.
How much is the Federal Battery Rebate worth?
Currently about $252 per usable kWh, so about $3,400 on a 13.5 kWh Tesla Powerwall 3 and about $7,050 on a 28 kWh Fox ESS. It falls to about $211 per usable kWh for installs from 1 January 2027, which is roughly $550 to $1,700 less depending on the size. It is deducted from your price and lodged by us.
Do I have to join a VPP to get the rebate?
No. The battery must be VPP-ready, but joining a VPP is optional. The NSW VPP incentive is a separate, optional bonus for signing up. We explain what the VPP pays and what it asks of your battery before you decide.
Can I add a battery to my existing solar?
In most cases yes, and the rebate applies. Fox ESS 28 kWh upgrades start from $8,090 and a Tesla Powerwall 3 from $9,900 after rebates. We check your inverter and switchboard on site and tell you straight if a retrofit or a replacement is the better value.
Will the battery keep my power on in a blackout?
Yes, if it is scoped for it. Every package we publish includes EPS backup, and we design whether it covers essential circuits or the whole house before you sign, so there are no surprises in the next storm.
Can I pay it off?
Yes. Coastal Solar Solutions is an approved vendor for the NSW Home Energy Saver Program: a 0% interest loan of up to $15,000 over up to 10 years for households with a combined taxable income of $210,000 or under, with no early-payout penalty. Rebates come off the bill before the loan applies. Ask during your free assessment and we will show you the weekly repayment against your current power bill.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Coastal Solar's installers are licensed electricians and every install meets Australian Standards and CEC requirements. [CONFIRM: licence numbers and public liability cover] Every job is done by our in-house crew, never subcontractors.
