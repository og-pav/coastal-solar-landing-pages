Questions people ask about EV charger installation

How much does an EV charger installation cost?
It depends on the charger, the cable run from your switchboard, single or three-phase supply and whether the switchboard needs work. We give one fixed price after seeing a photo of your switchboard and parking spot, and the quote is free.
Can I charge my car from solar only?
Yes. A Zappi in Eco+ mode only charges from your excess solar, and Tesla and Sigenergy chargers can be scheduled to run in the middle of the day. If you add a battery, you can charge overnight from solar stored during the day.
Do I need three-phase power?
No. A single-phase 7 kW charger adds roughly 40 km of range per hour, which fills most cars overnight. Three-phase allows up to 22 kW for faster charging. We check your supply and tell you what your switchboard can support before you spend anything on an upgrade.
Will my switchboard need an upgrade?
Sometimes, particularly on older homes. The charger runs on its own dedicated circuit and protection. We check the switchboard on the quote and include any work in the fixed price, so there is no surprise on the day.
Which charger should I get?
Zappi if you have solar and want to charge for close to nothing. Tesla Wall Connector for fast overnight charging and app control. Sigenergy if you are adding a SigenStor battery now or later and want the house, solar, battery and car in one app. We recommend one on the quote and explain why.
Can I pay it off?
Yes. Coastal Solar Solutions is an approved vendor for the NSW Home Energy Saver Program: a 0% interest loan of up to $15,000 over up to 10 years for households with a combined taxable income of $210,000 or under, with no early-payout penalty. Rebates come off the bill before the loan applies. Ask when you request your quote and we will show you the weekly repayment against your current power bill.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Coastal Solar's installers are licensed electricians and every install meets Australian Standards and CEC requirements. Every job is done by our in-house crew, never subcontractors.
