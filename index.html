Questions homeowners ask before they book

How much does solar and a battery cost?
Our published packages start from $9,720 after rebates for 6.65 kW of solar with a 14 kWh battery, and from $15,533 for our most popular 9.975 kW with 28 kWh. Every home is different, which is why the quote is free and done on site. You get a fixed price with the Federal Battery Rebate already deducted and no variations on the day.
Will the rebate really drop?
Yes. The Federal Battery Rebate is currently about $252 per usable kWh and is scheduled to fall to about $211 per usable kWh for systems installed from 1 January 2027. Booking your assessment now secures an install slot before the step-down.
Can I add a battery to my existing solar?
In most cases yes, and the rebate still applies. Battery upgrades start from $8,090 after rebates. We check your inverter and switchboard on site and tell you straight if a retrofit or a replacement is the better value. The assessment is free either way.
How long does installation take?
Most residential systems are installed in a single day by our own crew. We confirm your quote within 24 hours of the visit and your install date at booking.
Do I need to be home?
For the assessment, yes, so we can look at your switchboard and talk through your usage. For the install, someone needs to let us in and switch on with us at the end. Our own crew does both visits, so you deal with the same people from quote to switch-on.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Coastal Solar's installers are licensed electricians and every install meets Australian Standards and CEC requirements. Every job is done by our in-house crew, never subcontractors.
Do you offer finance?
Yes. Coastal Solar Solutions is an approved vendor for the NSW Home Energy Saver Program: a 0% interest loan of up to $15,000 over up to 10 years for households with a combined taxable income of $210,000 or under, with no early-payout penalty. Rebates come off the bill before the loan applies. Ask during your free assessment and we will show you the weekly repayment against your current power bill.
